Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account to use LovePDF?

No. All tools work without sign-up or login. Upload a file, use the tool, and download your result in one visit — no email, no password, no account required.

Is LovePDF really free?

Yes, every tool is free to use with no hidden charges, no watermarks added to your files, and no daily usage cap for normal personal use.

Are my files safe? Where do they go?

Your files are processed directly in your browser using your device's own processing power. They are never uploaded to our servers, which means we never see or store your documents.

What file formats are supported?

LovePDF supports PDF along with common conversion formats: JPG/PNG images, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), and PowerPoint (.pptx). Exact input/output formats vary by tool.

Can I use LovePDF on my phone?

Yes. The site works directly in your mobile browser — no app download needed. Every tool is built to work on both desktop and mobile.

Does compressing a PDF reduce quality?

Compression mainly optimizes embedded images and removes redundant data. Text stays sharp. Very high compression levels can slightly soften embedded photos, but text-heavy PDFs show little to no visible quality loss.

Can I merge PDFs in a specific order?

Yes. After uploading multiple files, drag to reorder them before merging — the final PDF follows the order you set.

Will converting PDF to Word keep my formatting?

The tool preserves layout, fonts, tables, and images as closely as possible, but very complex layouts may need minor manual adjustment afterward — a limitation of PDF-to-Word conversion in general.

What happens to my file after I close the tab?

If processing is local, your file exists only in that browser tab and is gone once you close it or refresh — nothing is stored anywhere.
